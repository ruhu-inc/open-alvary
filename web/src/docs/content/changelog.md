Changes that affect how you use the API and the MCP server, newest first.

## 21 October 2026

- The [OpenAPI document](https://api.open.alvary.ai/api/v1/openapi.json) names its server by its full address, `https://api.open.alvary.ai/api/v1`, no longer `/api/v1`, so a client generated from a saved copy calls the API.
- [MCP tools](/docs/api/mcp): a tool that runs and fails (not found, an answer too large, the Starter-rate allowance outside your plan used up, an invalid cursor) still answers a result with `isError: true` and its message, and now also gives the error code in `structuredContent.code`, with `retry_after_seconds` when you should wait. A refusal before a tool runs is a JSON-RPC error with the code in `error.data.code`, as before.
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence), [Search published texts](/docs/search#search-published-texts) and [Check cited parts](/docs/api/check-currentness) (also on MCP): a country missing from a treaty's party list is `treaty_not_in_force_for_state` only when the list is recorded as complete on the date; otherwise its `applicability.relation` is now `unknown`, and with `scope=applicable` the treaty is kept, labelled `unknown`, where it was left out before. A country the list names is answered by its own entry as before. See [the law that applies in a country](/docs/retrieval#the-law-that-applies-in-a-country).
- [Get a record](/docs/api/get-source) and [Get one part of a text exactly](/docs/api/get-anchor): a treaty's `treaty` gives `parties_complete_as_of`, the date its party list is complete as of, and `parties_complete_evidence`, the depositary's status list that shows it, once a reviewer has checked them. See [Regional instruments and treaties](/docs/records#regional-instruments-and-treaties).
- `scope=applicable` with a place within a country, such as `jurisdiction=ng-la`, also covers the country's own law (`ng`), labelled `national`, and the regional bodies and treaties of the country; another place's law in the country is `comparative`. Before, the country's law was not added and its relation was `unknown`. The answer's `applicability.within` names the country.
- Records of decrees and of executive, presidential and ministerial orders have `document_type` `regulation`, no longer `other`, as each is next published, so `type=regulation` finds them.
- [API keys](/docs/authentication#api-keys): rotating a key works even when you already have as many keys as allowed (10 personal keys per person, 20 shared keys per organisation). The new key takes the old one's place; the old one keeps working for 24 hours without being counted, and the console marks it **Replaced**. Rotating the new key within those 24 hours stops the old one at once.
- [Get a jurisdiction](/docs/api/get-jurisdiction) and [List jurisdictions](/docs/api/list-jurisdictions): a membership's `valid_from` and `valid_to` may be known only to a month (`2000-12`), a year (`1976`) or a window holding one day, unknown which (`[2016-08-15..2016-09-05]`, EDTF's "one of a set"); `valid_from_bounds` and `valid_to_bounds` give the earliest and latest day and the precision. A date within such a window lists the membership in the new `uncertain`, never in `items` or `ended`. Day dates are written as before.
- Each membership gives its `basis`: why it starts when it does (entry into force, deposit, admission decision, provisional application from signature, or signature), with the article that says so.
- Each membership lists the country's `suspensions` from taking part in the body's activities, with `in_effect` on `as_of`. Each article of a suspension's `legal_basis` says why it is cited: `decision_basis`, the article the decision itself cites, or `treaty_rule`, the treaty's rule the suspension applies. A suspended country stays in `items`: a suspension never ends a membership. The change feed's new type `suspension_recorded` records a suspension, its lifting (`change_reason` `lifted`) or a correction; a body in the `jurisdiction` filter matches its members' suspensions. `membership_recorded` changes carry the dates' bounds and the `basis`. See [Find which bodies a country belongs to](/docs/records#find-which-bodies-a-country-belongs-to).
- [Get a court register](/docs/api/get-court-register) (`GET /api/v1/courts/{jurisdiction}`) and [List court registers](/docs/api/list-court-registers) (`GET /api/v1/courts`): a jurisdiction's courts with their levels and appeal routes, how each court's decisions bear on the others, and whether precedent binds there, every entry with its sources. Regional and continental bodies (`aa-ohada`, `aa-eac`, `aa-ecowas`, `aa-au`) also give the states their courts reach, routes from national courts and declarations admitting individual cases. Until a reviewer has checked a register, every `relation` is `unknown`.
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence): a decision's unit names its court in `authority.court`, and once its register is reviewed, `authority.court.bears_on` says how the court's decisions bear on other courts, with the sources (also on MCP). See [Court authority](/docs/retrieval#court-authority).
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence) and [Search published texts](/docs/api/search-text) take `scope=applicable` with one `jurisdiction` (also MCP `retrieve_evidence` and `search_text`): the search then also covers the instruments of the African Union and the regional bodies the country belonged to on `as_of`, from reviewed memberships only, and the treaties it ratified or acceded to. Each unit and passage says how it relates to the country (`applicability.relation`: `national`, `regional_direct`, `regional_requires_transposition`, `regional_binding_on_addressees`, `treaty_in_force_for_state`, `treaty_not_in_force_for_state`, `non_binding`, `comparative` or `unknown`), with what that rests on; the answer's `applicability` states what was added and left out. Without `scope`, answers are unchanged. See [the law that applies in a country](/docs/retrieval#the-law-that-applies-in-a-country).
- [Check cited parts](/docs/api/check-currentness) (also MCP `check_currentness`): an item may give a `jurisdiction`, and its result then says whether the record binds there on the date (`applicability`: `bound`, `not_bound` or `unknown`).
- [Get a record](/docs/api/get-source) and [Get one part of a text exactly](/docs/api/get-anchor): a regional instrument has `regional` (its organ, legal effect, member states bound and transposition deadline), and a treaty has `treaty` (its parties, each with dates of signature, ratification, accession and denunciation, reservations and domesticating acts). Evidence units carry both, a treaty without its parties. See [Regional instruments and treaties](/docs/records#regional-instruments-and-treaties).
- [Get a court register](/docs/api/get-court-register) (`GET /api/v1/courts/{jurisdiction}`) and [List court registers](/docs/api/list-court-registers) (`GET /api/v1/courts`): a jurisdiction's courts with their levels and appeal routes, how each court's decisions bear on the others, and whether precedent binds there, every entry with its sources. A relation is `binding`, `prevails_on_matter` (precedence on a subject matter, not stare decisis), `binding_in_referred_case` (the ruling binds only the court that referred the question, in that case), `persuasive` or `not_applicable`, and quotes the words it rests on in `rests_on`. Regional and continental bodies (`aa-ohada`, `aa-eac`, `aa-ecowas`, `aa-au`) also give the states their courts reach, routes from national courts and declarations admitting individual cases. Each entry has its own review (`review_state`, `review_level`, `reviewed_by`, `reviewer_role`, `reviewed_on`): `structural` for courts, matters, members, routes and declarations, `doctrinal` for relations and the precedent rule, which states the reviewer's legal qualification (`reviewer_qualification`). A relation is `unknown` until it has its own doctrinal review and the courts it names have theirs; a `binding` or `persuasive` one also waits for the precedent rule's review, a `not_applicable` one (a court in another hierarchy) does not.
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence): a decision's unit names its court in `authority.court`, and `authority.court.bears_on` lists each reviewed relation of the court's decisions to other courts, with its sources and their words (also on MCP). `court_relation` ranks `binding` decisions first, then `persuasive` ones; a ruling binding only in the case referred ranks as `unknown`. See [Court authority](/docs/retrieval#court-authority).
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence) and [Resolve a citation](/docs/api/resolve-citation): an article numbered after another is read by its printed number (`art. 2-1`, `article 626-1-1-1`), and where a text holds an article's alinéas as parts of their own, a cited alinéa resolves to that alinéa, given back as `art. 11, al. 2`.
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence) and [Resolve a citation](/docs/api/resolve-citation): French citations are read too. An article's alinéa is read as its `(2)` (`article 10, alinéa 2`, `art. 10 al. 2`), and the law may follow `de`, `du`, `de la`, `de l'` or `des` (`article 10, alinéa 2, de l'Acte uniforme …`). A citation is no longer also read as a shorter pinpoint in a law named by the rest (`article 10` in a law called `alinéa 2`).
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence): because citations are read differently, the routing rules are now `oa-route-3` (`revisions.router`, also on MCP). Answers you cached under `oa-route-2` may route a citation differently now.
- [Search](/docs/api/search) (`GET /api/v1/search`, MCP `search_catalogue`) and the website's search match **whole words** from an index, no longer any run of letters. Every word must be in the record, in any order (it was a phrase, in order); `invest` no longer finds `Investments`. Words are looked for in a record's summary too, and in one part of a cleared text rather than anywhere in it. See [Find records](/docs/search#search).
- Search answers may have `notices`: why published texts weren't searched (the index is being built, or that search took too long), or that the query has no letters or digits. A query of punctuation alone now finds nothing instead of matching it.
- [Get a record's editions](/docs/api/get-expressions) (`GET /api/v1/sources/{id}/expressions`, and the new MCP tool `list_expressions`): each edition's `authority` is now what the record states (`authoritative`, `equally_authentic`, `official_translation`, `unofficial_translation`, `machine_translation`, or `unknown` when nothing is recorded), with `authority_evidence` saying where it is stated. The answer adds `language_rule`: the law's own rule for which version prevails where its language versions differ. See [Language editions](/docs/records#language-editions).
- [Get a record's texts](/docs/api/get-versions) and [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence): each text and each unit carries its edition's `expression_authority`, with `expression_authority_evidence` when recorded.
- [Search published texts](/docs/search#search-published-texts) and [Retrieve evidence](/docs/retrieval): Portuguese and Spanish texts are matched by word stems with their common words left out, and Arabic texts by word stems with every word kept (`analysis` gives `portuguese`, `spanish` or `arabic`). Other languages, such as Kiswahili, are still matched word for word, and both answers say so in their notices, now in the same words. The analysis is `oa-search-5`.
- [Get a record's history](/docs/api/get-relationships) (also MCP `get_relationships`) lists more kinds of checked link: `commences`/`commenced_by`, `transposes`/`transposed_by`, `domesticates`/`domesticated_by`, `implements`/`implemented_by`, `ratifies`/`ratified_by` and `cites`/`cited_by`. See [what each kind means](/docs/records#find-what-replaced-a-law).
- `legal_effect_recorded` now also records commencements, transpositions, domestications and ratifications; for the last three each record has its own change, the law's naming the instrument in `legal_effect.of`. A withdrawn link, or a withdrawn event on provisions, corrects the change that recorded it (`review_state` `withdrawn`).
- [Get a jurisdiction](/docs/api/get-jurisdiction) (`GET /api/v1/jurisdictions/{id}?as_of=`): a country's memberships of the African Union and the regional bodies on a date, or a body's members. Each gives its first and last day, its evidence and who reviewed it. `status` is `unknown` until a membership has been reviewed. See [Find which bodies a country belongs to](/docs/records#find-which-bodies-a-country-belongs-to).
- [List jurisdictions](/docs/api/list-jurisdictions) gives each country's `member_of` in the same form, takes `as_of`, and lists the regional bodies a membership names. A regional body's `country_code` is null.
- The change feed's new type `membership_recorded` records a membership being added, ending (`change_reason` `ended`) or being corrected. A `jurisdiction` filter for a body such as `aa-ecowas` matches changes to its members.

## 19 October 2026

- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence): each unit now gives the pages of the official file it is on (`page_start`, `page_end`), that file's hash and when it was fetched (`original_sha256`, `retrieved_at`), and `links` to read it on Open Alvary or by the API. Questions may cite a part as people write it (`section 23 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act`, `NDPA s. 23`, `Part V of the NDPA`), ask for a page (`page 18 of the NDPA`) or be an Open Alvary or official address; `source_id` reads a pinpoint alone within one record (also on MCP). Routes `link` and `page` are new (`oa-route-2`); the ranking profile is `oa-rank-3`.
- [Retrieve evidence](/docs/api/retrieve-evidence) (`GET /api/v1/retrieve`, MCP `retrieve_evidence`): ranked parts of published texts for a question, each with its exact words, citation, hashes, currentness, definitions and why it ranks there. See [Retrieve evidence for AI answers](/docs/retrieval).
- [Check cited parts](/docs/api/check-currentness) (`POST /api/v1/currentness`, MCP `check_currentness`): whether cited parts are still published with the same words, and whether a newer text exists.
- [Download a bulk release file](/docs/api/get-bulk-file) (`GET /api/v1/bulk/…`): releases with full text, with a key, for organisations whose access includes bulk releases (granted by agreement). [List data releases](/docs/api/list-releases) lists them only to those organisations, for their plan's countries. Catalogue releases still download from `/downloads/…` with no key.

## 17 October 2026

- These docs: guides, a reference for every route generated from the API's own description, and every page also as Markdown (add `.md` to its address) and in [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt).
- Every refusal from `/api/v1` is now a problem document with a code, including a malformed parameter (`400 invalid_input`), an unknown record (`404 unavailable`) and an answer too large (`413 result_too_large`).
- The OpenAPI document describes every route, parameter, field and error.
- The MCP tools describe their arguments.
- A change's `work.api_url` links the record at `https://api.open.alvary.ai/api/v1/sources/<id>`.
- `/api/v1/releases` gives each release's files at `/downloads/…`, where they download.

## 16 October 2026

- The REST API at `https://api.open.alvary.ai/api/v1` and the MCP server at `https://api.open.alvary.ai/mcp` open, with API keys and sign-in for AI tools, in the console at `https://open.alvary.ai/console`.
- The free Starter plan for everyone; other plans on request.
- Monitors with an inbox and email digests; webhooks on paid plans.
- The anonymous API at `https://open.alvary.ai/api` closes and answers `410 gone`, except the change feed, which stays open.
