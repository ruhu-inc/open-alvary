What is checked?

Check a legal quote against Open Alvary's published text

Name a law and its section, paste the exact words, and get a signed check that this browser verifies. No AI or key is needed. With your own AI key you can also ask questions, and each quote in the answer is checked the same way.

    The agents behind Ask AI

    Ask AI is three agents that run in your browser, on the AI provider and key you choose:

    1. Law desk talks with you and writes the answer.
    2. Finder searches the published laws and copies the exact words, using Open Alvary's tools.
    3. Verifier sends each quote to Open Alvary's check. Code on Open Alvary's server, not an AI, compares the words and signs a receipt.

    Each answer lists the steps the agents took. Their instructions are public, like a page's source: law_desk. Other agent sites can call Open Alvary's law_check network by its address, https://open.alvary.ai/agents/check/law_check, and it runs on their visitor's own key.

    Ask AI works best with a model that calls tools well. Qwen3.8 Flash on Otari is suggested. Checking a quote needs no AI at all.

    Asking a question needs your own AI key.
